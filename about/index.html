ABOUT

Technical leader.
Hands-on by design.

I help turn emerging technical capability into enterprise systems that organizations can operate, govern and adopt.

Across more than eleven years in technology, I have worked across domains while staying close to architecture, implementation and the people operating the result. I lead through technical judgment, clear system boundaries and enough hands-on depth to carry decisions into reality.

Forward deployment is part of how I operate: discovering the real workflow, making architecture and model choices, integrating into the operating environment, measuring what users experience and adapting after contact with reality.

Earlier work in accessibility, multilingual interfaces and constrained connectivity led to peer-reviewed IEEE and ACM publications and participation as a W3C AI & Accessibility panelist.
